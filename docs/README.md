# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through an iterative lifecycle: initiate work by validating the business need, stakeholders, and success measures; plan scope, backlog, milestones, resources, risks, and dependencies; execute in small increments with regular tracking, testing, and reviews; release with deployment checks and rollback plans; and close with a retrospective that turns learnings into owned improvement actions. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, and communications, while Product Managers own outcomes, prioritization, and success measures. Developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders contribute to implementation, validation, and decisions.

Teams use project boards and clear acceptance criteria to keep delivery visible. Communication includes team standups, regular PM and Product Manager alignment, weekly delivery updates, milestone demos, and stakeholder reports. Risks and dependencies are tracked and reviewed regularly, with escalation paths for blockers and incidents. Quality is built into the workflow through unit and integration testing where appropriate, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, CI tests and linting, security scanning, code review, and manual QA when needed. Releases require readiness checks, post-deployment verification, and rollback or mitigation plans; retrospectives turn lessons into prioritized, measurable actions.
